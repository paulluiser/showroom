# Liquid Horizon

## Overview

The original variant 3 is the selected visual world. The later generated full-screen mockups were rejected and are not implementation targets. A live liquid landscape gives the hero its identity; restrained editorial sections make professional evidence easy to read. Headerless throughout.

## Colors

Midnight ground and warmer white text with cyan, blue, violet, and magenta reserved for media and selected states. Keep backgrounds quiet behind paragraphs. Secondary body text #B4BDD4.

## Typography

Self-hosted Sora for headlines and Manrope for body. Hero42–78px desktop; mobile33–44px. Section titles34–55px. General body14–16px; secondary labels12–14px. Balance line breaks rather than shrinking long titles.

## Shapes

## Components

- Role selectors reveal after scrolling; manual selection is held, announced politely, and resumed explicitly.
- Timeline progress fills as it enters the viewport. Active career nodes illuminate; responsibilities expand with a persistent focusable control.
- Native section anchors preserve deep links and browser navigation. Mobile bottom navigation remains labeled and touch-friendly.
- The small visitor guide defaults collapsed. Expanded guide carries mobile motion controls.
- Pause and OS reduced motion stop decorative animation and automatic role changes. The timeline shows a complete static path under reduced motion.
- WebGL continuous rendering suspends when paused, hidden, or outside the viewport. Keep canvas dimensions responsive and cap device pixel ratio.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Preserve calm reading surfaces and a clear title–summary–detail hierarchy.
- Use illustration only where it explains relationships; omit icons that repeat a label.
- Keep data truthful: no invented tools, clients, outcomes, or HRIS modules.
- Target IT Project Management and Product Manager/Owner opportunities without implying prior product-role employment.
- Do not use redirect-arrow decoration or a persistent mobile orb dock.
